tasteq listening study

Teach an equalizer your taste

You will hear short excerpts in pairs and pick the one that sounds better to you. A model learns your preferred sound from your answers, first on simple test sounds, then on songs from styles you like. At the end, a blind test checks whether it really learned.

Before you start

  • Headphones recommended. Use a quiet place. Each pair is matched in loudness, so pick what sounds better, not louder.
  • What we collect: your A/B answers and how long you listened, the choices on these screens (study length, device, music styles) and a short questionnaire at the end. No name, e-mail, IP address or account.
  • Anonymous code. You get a random code (like K7QF-2MZD). It is the only link to your answers; keep it if you want to resume or have your data removed.
  • You can stop at any time with “Leave study”. You choose whether to keep or delete what you answered.
  • Use of the data: anonymous answers are used for research on personalised audio and may be published as an open dataset. Legal basis: your consent (LGPD art. 7, I / GDPR art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.
